(02) Meet the studio

We make games with flavour.

I’m SZE, a product designer and co-founder of Oupelaye Studio. I work across game concepts, UX, interaction design, art direction, and playtesting—usually in close collaboration with a developer.

I care about the moment a player understands the hook: the interface disappears, the system clicks, and curiosity takes over.
