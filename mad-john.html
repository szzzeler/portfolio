Case study / 01

Mad John

Making an original card-and-puzzle game immediately learnable through play.

RoleGame UX · Art direction · Co-creatorDuration3 months · OngoingToolsGodot · GitHub · AI-assisted prototyping
Overview

Mad John combines card and puzzle mechanics for casual play during family gatherings and shared downtime.

The idea

A new mechanic only works when players understand its hook quickly.

I originated the game concept and led the initial execution with AI-assisted coding. I shape game UX and art direction while collaborating with a developer who implements the game logic. Together we use Godot and GitHub to build, test, and track the work.

Playtest-led iteration
01

Test across experience levels

Played with six people: three with gaming experience and three older adults without gaming experience.

02

Make the first move clear

Early feedback showed that the instructions needed clarity, so I added a hands-on tutorial that teaches the interaction in context.

03

Refine the balance

Players understood the core hook and continued playing. The remaining focus is fine-tuning scoring and gameplay balance.

Gameplay direction

Give players feedback they can act on.

The interface keeps the active hand, score target, multiplier, power-ups, and end-of-round breakdown visible while preserving the game’s distinctive visual world.

Current status

A validated core loop, clearer onboarding, and final gameplay tuning underway before release.
